# Heliarchy — 10-Turn Early Game Playthrough Guide (Years 2400–2410)

For a ~20-minute test session, run at **`15x (Blitz)`** (`1 Game Year = 2 real minutes`), or bump to **`60x`** (`30 real seconds per Year`) to fast-forward long interstellar flights.

---

## Year 2400 (Opening Decisions)
- **Survey Your Home System:** You already know all 3–6 planets orbiting your home star. Toggle the **`Habitability`** and **`Germanium %`** overlays (or click each planet in the Inspector) to see if you have a green (`> 0%`) sister planet or a rich mineral rock right in your own solar system.
- **Set Homeworld Production:** Your Homeworld starts with `35,000` pop (`Cap: 35` operable structures), `10` Mines, `10` Factories, and `~45 Res/yr`. Queue **`Factories` (`10R, 4Ge`)** and **`Mines` (`5R`)** to grow your industrial base.
- **Launch Your First Scout Tour:** Select **`Long Range Scout #1`** (`3.6 ly/yr` at `Burn 5`). Hold **`Shift + Right-Click`** on 2–3 nearby stars, set **`Burn 3–4`** + **`Final: Flyby Coast`** (or `Burn 5` Orbit Transfer for a single star), and click **Launch Tour**.
- **Decide on `Santa Maria #2` (Colony Ship):**
  - *If you have a green sister planet in-system:* Right-click it to hop over in **25–90 sim seconds** (`2–6 real seconds` at `15x`) and click **Found Colony**.
  - *If not:* Hold `Santa Maria #2` in orbit until your Scout reports back.

---

## Years 2401–2402 (First Production & First Telemetry)
- **Build More Scouts:** Once your first couple of Factories finish, queue **`1–2 Scout Probes` (`25R`)** at your Homeworld Starbase so you can scout in multiple directions at once.
- **First Contact with Neighbor Stars:** `Long Range Scout #1` reaches its first target star, emits a cyan **Telemetry Signal** (`0.85 ly/sim min`, ~5–6 sim minutes to cross 4.5 ly), and executes its **Gravity-Assist Slingshot** toward star #2.
- **Watch Your New Sister Colony (if founded):** Check its **Worker Cap (`Cap: 25`)**—it starts with `5` Mines and `5` Factories, so queue Mines (`5R`, `0` minerals) first to start extracting local minerals without spending Germanium.

---

## Years 2403–2405 (First Interstellar Colony & the Germanium Pinch)
- **Read the Incoming Telemetry:** As telemetry packets reach your Homeworld, toggle **`Habitability`** and **`Mineral Abundance`** to compare the newly charted neighbor systems.
- **Launch Your First Interstellar Colony Ship:** Send **`Santa Maria #2`** (or a newly built `Colony Ship` at `65R`) to the best green planet in a neighboring system. Colony Ships cruise at **`1.65 ly/yr` (`Burn 5`)**, so a 4.5 ly crossing takes **~2.7 Game Years**.
- **Watch the Germanium Bottleneck:** Your Homeworld starts with `165 kT` Germanium. Building Factories (`4Ge` each), Scouts (`4Ge`), and Colony Ships (`15Ge`) will start testing your Germanium mining rate—making a high-`Germanium %` planet in-system or next door a prime mining target.

---

## Years 2406–2408 (Logistics, Colonist Shuttling & Probe Attrition)
- **Build a `Medium Freighter` (`45R`):** Launch it to your Homeworld or mineral-rich sister planet and open **`Transfer Cargo (Sliders)...`** to:
  - **Export Colonists** from your growing Homeworld to boost smaller colonies' **Worker Caps** (bringing idle Mines/Factories online).
  - **Haul Germanium & Ironium** back to your Homeworld Starbase so ship production never stalls.
- **Monitor Deep-Space Probe Power:** Scouts flying `> 3.5 ly` past your nearest colony will experience **Deep-Space Power Drain** and eventually go **`Derelict (0% Pwr)`** after charting 3–5 stars—marking the natural limit of your first wave of exploration.

---

## Years 2409–2410 (End of Early Game Checkpoint)
By **Year 2410**, a healthy opening puts you at:
- **2–4 Colonies** (Homeworld + 1–2 in-system worlds + 1–2 neighbor-star colonies)
- **3–5 Active Fleets** (Scouts mapping the 10–15 ly perimeter, a Freighter running logistics, and **`Stalwart #3`** patrolling)
- **Frontier Decisions:** If your probes have spotted the rival **Xenon** system (`~6–16 ly` away), your next milestone is building an **Orbital Starbase (`100R` + minerals)** on a forward colony to extend your refueling/power grid and constructing **Destroyers (`90R`)**.
