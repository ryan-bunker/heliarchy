# Cohort Spawning, Salvage & Catch-Up Mechanics

**Core Architecture:** Clustered Frontier Spawning & Asymmetric Tech Progression

**Design Goal:** Prevent player isolation during slow signup periods in a 1:1 Milky Way-scale galaxy, while enabling newcomers to bridge multi-year technology gaps without invalidating the infrastructural and industrial achievements of veteran empires.

## 1. Clustered Frontier Spawning ("Civilized Bubbles")

* **Concept:** Concentrate player arrivals into active **Colonized Bubbles** along the galactic arms rather than scattering trickle signups across the entire 100+ billion star galaxy, pairing proximity with economic protections.

* **Gameplay Impact:**

  * **Cohort Wave Spawning:** Group all new players joining over a multi-month window (e.g., 3–6 months) into the same 30–50 light-year frontier pocket—ensuring even slow-trickle arrivals immediately have peer neighbors for trade, diplomacy, and early skirmishes.

  * **Immediate Signs of Life:** New commanders spawn close enough to established regions that passive long-range receivers pick up faint, delayed radio chatter and drive plumes on day one, eliminating the "empty single-player void" feel.

  * **Asymmetric Starter Economics:** Starter systems contain abundant bulk metals (Iron, Boranium, Germanium) for rapid early bootstrapping, but zero rare exotic isotopes—making conquering a new player's homeworld economically unprofitable compared to the fuel cost of deploying a veteran armada.

  * **Protectorate Incentives:** Reward veterans for building comms relays and defensive patrols over adjacent frontier pockets via system-generated **Trade & Transit Bonuses** tied to the survival and growth of neighboring starter worlds.

## 2. Derelict Salvage & Field-Stripped Hulls

* **Concept:** Persistent space combat and ancient frontier graveyards leave behind physical debris fields drifting along inertial vectors, acting as high-yield progression accelerators for smaller empires.

* **Gameplay Impact:**

  * **Two-Tier Salvage Choices:** When a salvage tug reaches a high-tech wreck, players choose between:

    1. **Scrap for Applied Research:** Dismantle the debris on-site for an immediate, massive injection of research points directly into the corresponding *Stars!* tech category.

    2. **Tow & Refit (Prototype Usage):** Haul the damaged hull to a drydock and patch it into service—allowing a newcomer to operate a high-tier flagship long before they can manufacture one, at the cost of higher fuel and maintenance overhead.

  * **Frontier "Graveyard" Nebulae:** Seed starter clusters with ancient AI battlefields and derelict generation ships so lone trickle-joining players have reliable salvage targets and localized NPC threats while awaiting human neighbors.

## 3. Reverse Engineering & Empirical Telemetry

* **Concept:** Model the real-world reality that once an advanced technology is demonstrated publicly, competitors can reverse-engineer it far faster than the original inventor developed it.

* **Gameplay Impact:**

  * **Combat & "Black Box" Data:** Surviving an engagement against a higher-tech fleet—or recovering courier drones from a skirmish—generates **Empirical Combat Data** on observed enemy weapons, drives, and shields. Feeding this into research labs grants a $2\times$–$4\times$ research speed multiplier until reaching parity with the observed tech level.

  * **Signal Intelligence (SIGINT) Leakage:** Pointing passive scanners at active veteran relay networks or high-energy drive plumes slowly accumulates background research bonuses toward those demonstrated propulsion and electronics tiers.

## 4. Asymptotic Tech Curves & Veteran Moats

* **Concept:** Separate **baseline scientific parity** (which newcomers can reach quickly) from **industrial scale and blueprint mastery** (which protect multi-year veteran investment).

* **Gameplay Impact:**

  * **Logarithmic Power Scaling:** Structure *Stars!* tech categories on an exponential research cost curve with diminishing combat returns—ensuring a Tech Level 12 fleet is ~75–80% as effective in raw combat math as a Tech Level 20 fleet, keeping mid-tier fleets relevant in coalition warfare.

  * **Proprietary "Masterwork" Tuning:** Salvage and reverse engineering only unlock *standard* component blueprints. Veterans who invest deep research time past baseline thresholds unlock **Blueprint Tuning** (e.g., custom thermal efficiency, reduced hull mass, or specialized stealth profiles) that cannot be reverse-engineered from wrecks.

  * **Infrastructural Moat:** Even when a newcomer bridges the tech gap, veterans retain decisive strategic advantages through established **subspace relay highways, terraformed forge worlds, and deep-space fuel depot networks** that take months of logistical planning to construct.
